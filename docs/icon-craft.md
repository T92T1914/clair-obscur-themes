# Individual icon refinement, artwork 0.30.0

Artwork 0.30.0 refines 184 of the 300 existing drawings and retains 116. Both appearances were compared individually at 24, 32, 48, 64 and 128 CSS pixels, with original alternatives and independent drawing review. The catalogue still contains 276 named apps and 24 general functions. All IDs, labels, package hints and Android mappings remain unchanged.

The principal change is app identity. A tidy category symbol can still be the wrong drawing for a particular app. Banking buildings, generic video tools and weather symbols gave way to more specific structures where the publisher reference supported them. Construction corrections also separate small counters, remove projecting seams and keep deliberate shared joins distinct from accidental tangencies.

Zillow and Zillow Rentals intentionally share one monochrome mark because their inspected publisher imagery distinguishes those listings through color. Their separate IDs, labels and assignments remain intact. Tiny wordmarks and similar C/P silhouettes still benefit from labels. Agent review does not establish human recognition, preference or universal legibility.

WEBTOON retains a comic-panel interpretation and The Weather Channel retains a day/night monitor. Neither reproduces its publisher's current word badge. Final unlabeled comparison kept that identity limitation visible. Their clear functional constructions were retained because replacing them with tiny multi-line lettering had no demonstrated advantage. These are deliberate compromises, not evidence that every named app is recognizable without its label.

## Construction and references

The family keeps its 512-unit artboard, 32-unit tile inset, 448-unit tile and 104-unit corner radius. Black marks on white Clair tiles and white marks on black Obscur tiles share geometry and transparent outer margins. The 16-unit base stroke remains, with deliberate local weight, filled forms and optical adjustments where the identifying shape needs them. The 184 refined drawings are maintained as original geometric source in `themeforge/icon_craft.py`; retained drawings remain in the existing generators. No publisher pixels, other icon-pack paths or font files are imported.

Official app listings in the catalogue supplied bounded identity references. The [Arcticons construction guide](https://docs.arcticons.com/contribute/creating-icons), [Lucide principles](https://lucide.dev/contribute/icons/design-principles) and [Samsung symbol guidance](https://developer.samsung.com/one-ui/iconography/symbol.html) informed comparisons. Their grids and rules belong to their own systems. The companion consumes mapped PNG resources, so [Android adaptive-icon guidance](https://developer.android.com/develop/ui/compose/system/icon_design_adaptive) is context rather than a claim that these resources implement adaptive layers.

The retained literature review informed questions about familiarity, meaningfulness, complexity and visual search. It did not inspect these drawings or select a universally best style. Abstract-only and full-text access were recorded separately in the private research packet. No aesthetic guarantee or recognition percentage is inferred from that literature.

## Assets and consumer checks

The current archive contains 600 SVG masters. Exactly 368 SVGs change from 0.29.0 and the other 232 retain their bytes. The complete 0.29.0 archive and all 28 earlier archives remain separately available. The existing exporter creates 1,200 PNGs, covering both appearances at 512 and 1024 pixels. The maintained source identity now includes the new drawing module in the existing export and Android build guards.

Clair Icons 0.1.3/code 4 and Obscur Icons 0.1.14/code 15 select the 0.30.0 artwork through the existing separately identified packages. Signed packages remain private and require their own compiled-resource and signer checks. The source gallery, decoded exports, compiled packages and served download checks are distinct evidence layers.

Phone installation and application are deferred. Current launcher identities, Theme Park refresh, masks, cover and inner displays, coexistence, switching, persistence and restoration need native qualification. This artwork change does not change the keyboard backgrounds, phone font, watch face or accepted desktop packages. The [0.29.0 art-direction record](art-direction.md) and [0.28.0 identity record](icon-identity.md) remain historical records of those revisions.
