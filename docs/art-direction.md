# Art direction: specific app cues in a restrained family

This record describes artwork 0.29.0. The later [complete icon refinement](icon-craft.md) reevaluates every drawing while preserving this revision and its evidence.

Artwork 0.29.0 refines five existing assignments while keeping the black and white family. Delta, American, Southwest, Wikipedia and Earth previously relied on general travel or reference metaphors. Their selected drawings preserve more specific app cues through a controlled mixture of outlines, fill and open space. The catalogue still has 276 named apps and 24 general functions. Labels, package identities, tile construction and mappings remain unchanged.

Clair uses black marks on white tiles. Obscur reverses that relationship, with neutral gray for subordinate information in the wider preview. Interface text requests locally installed Inter with readable fallbacks. A specific app letterform, such as Wikipedia's W, can remain an original icon construction rather than becoming an Inter glyph. No font binary is included in these icon or keyboard downloads.

## What was compared

The initial overview covered all 300 choices in both appearances at 64 CSS pixels across twelve catalogue sheets. That located repeated category metaphors and apparent weight differences. It was an overview at one size, not a small-size review of every drawing.

The selected group then received accepted, outline, solid and hybrid comparisons at actual 24, 32, 48, 64 and 128 CSS pixels in both appearances. Mixed neighbors, labeled and varied-order unlabeled views helped separate silhouette and apparent weight from the name printed below a drawing. United Airlines, Browser, Chrome, Claude and Perplexity remained neighboring controls. ChatGPT's earlier five-round comparison was retained rather than reopened without a new reason.

An independent reviewer who did not draw the candidates inspected the comparisons and keyboard references. The first Southwest outline and hybrid had seam ends projecting beyond the heart contour. That result was retained as a rejected alternative. A focused correction to the outline endpoints was inspected again at all five sizes in both appearances before selection.

The circle and rounded-square compositions were static 64-pixel clipping illustrations on a neutral background. They do not establish adaptive-icon layers, the installed Theme Park mask or physical-phone appearance. Reviewer judgments with known app targets are not human recognition or preference measurements.

## Construction rules

Start with the existing 512-unit artboard, tile inset of 32 units, 448-unit tile, 104-unit corner radius and 16-unit baseline stroke. Those are this pack's conventions. They are not universal ratios or Samsung import requirements.

Preserve the distinguishing silhouette and meaningful gaps before adding detail. Compare apparent size and density beside real neighbors, since equal bounding boxes do not create equal visual weight. Filled anchors can justify a different optical footprint, but a filled form must earn that exception through a visible identity cue.

Keep joins, caps, curves and diagonal partitions deliberate. Inspect narrow valleys, intersections and seam ends at actual small sizes. Do not replace those inspections with a large construction image. Equivalent geometry serves Clair and Obscur, while each appearance still needs visual inspection because opposite backgrounds can change perceived weight.

Use existing monochrome roles and preserve transparent margins and accepted containers. Keep app labels and exact package hints available for assignment. An appealing generic plane or globe can still lose an app-specific cue, and a dense center can sometimes be part of that cue. Neither minimum line count nor uniform fill is an automatic goal.

## What the references contributed

The following sources were inspected on October 27, 2026. Each supplies a bounded design question or construction method.

- [Arcticons at commit `4e0bf8a`](https://github.com/Arcticons-Team/Arcticons/tree/4e0bf8a8c2e85372b0c2a3f8583fb58dee70b050) and its [construction guide](https://docs.arcticons.com/contribute/creating-icons) show how a monochrome line family can retain specific app cues with few meaningful lines. Three pinned SVG examples, Wikipedia, Earth and United, were source-inspected rather than raster-reviewed. The pack's 48-pixel grid, one-pixel stroke and paths were not imported. Its pinned README distinguishes GPL-3.0 code and CC BY-SA 4.0 icons, so its artwork is not a pool of replacement product paths.
- [Lucide's construction principles](https://lucide.dev/contribute/icons/design-principles) support optical centering, clear gaps, consistent joins and comparison of apparent weight. Its literal 24-pixel grid and two-pixel stroke belong to an interface-symbol set. Its [brand-logo policy](https://lucide.dev/brand-logo-statement) also makes clear that those interface symbols serve a different purpose from branded launcher assignments.
- [Samsung's symbol guidance](https://developer.samsung.com/one-ui/iconography/symbol.html) supplies a useful counterpoint for simple, complete forms and consistent weight. The [January 23, 2025 One UI 7 narrative](https://design.samsung.com/global/contents/one-ui-7/) supports retaining key app elements while differentiating them. Its color, glass and gradient presentation was not adopted as this monochrome family's direction or treated as evidence of native behavior.
- [Nothing OS 2.0's presentation](https://whatsnew-phone1.nothing.tech/) is a dated coherence reference for icons, labels and spacing. The [2023 rollout announcement](https://nothing.community/d/1424-nothing-os-20-comes-to-phone-1) places that direction in its launch context. It does not establish current Samsung capabilities or authorize importing Nothing's assets or fonts.
- [Android's adaptive-icon documentation](https://developer.android.com/develop/ui/compose/system/icon_design_adaptive) describes layers and launcher-dependent masks. This companion supplies mapped bitmap artwork through a different mechanism. A clipping illustration cannot replace an actual consumer check.

Two supplied research records were fetched through Consensus and checked against their original publisher abstracts. Full methods were not accessed. [Shen and colleagues' 2025 outline/solid study](https://doi.org/10.1002/hfm.70006) reports 27 participants, 120 icon pairs and two familiarization sessions. Its solid-form advantage in the tested recognition/search conditions, especially at lower familiarity, supported comparing fill here. It did not choose the winning drawings or establish aesthetic superiority.

[Isherwood, McDougall and Curry's 2007 identification study](https://doi.org/10.1518/001872007X200102) reports thirty participants across repeated trials, with icon-function association and familiarity contributing differently over learning. That supports preserving specific identity and treating unfamiliar identification separately from learned recognition. The retained report's older 197-choice inventory remains historical context. Neither abstract establishes how people will perform with this 300-choice collection.

## Keyboard hierarchy

The maintained keyboard illustration gives letters the main visual weight. A quieter number row and outlined utility keys reduce the repeated equal-weight boxes, while bold text and an underline identify an example suggestion. The grouped-header alternative added an unnecessary frame. The selected reference retains visible Shift, Backspace, mode, Space, punctuation and Enter, along with labeled pressed, selected, focus and unavailable examples.

The illustration and its maintained HTML recipe must stay within the host's controls. Gboard's optional PNG changes background pixels only. Key geometry, individual key colors, suggestions, Enter accents, live fonts and correction remain Gboard-owned. Use its supported native plain theme where suitable. For Samsung Keyboard, apply only background or key colors actually exposed by the installed Theme Park or Keys Cafe version, retain native state cues and keep a separate draft for restoration.

The example selected suggestion does not imply replacement after Space or Enter. A browser request for local Inter does not establish phone font availability or live glyph use. Preserve the current font, language, layouts, learned words and correction settings. Keyboard Companion 0.1.0, both original background PNGs, its bundled guide and all nine downloadable outputs remain unchanged. There is no new keyboard bundle or font package.
