## Compared drawings

Each target had an accepted baseline, a conservative vector alternative and a more distinctive construction. Saved comparisons used 24, 32, 48, 64 and 128 CSS pixels in Clair and Obscur, with labels, unchanged neighbors and an unlabeled review view. Enlarged construction views were inspected separately. These were agent design reviews with known targets, not a blinded participant study or a measurement of recognition.

| Target | Decision | Visible reason and tradeoff |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ChatGPT | Retain the accepted bubble and balanced sparkle | Linked-loop alternatives became dense flowers, honeycomb, an atom-like construction or a mesh. They did not give a sufficient reason to replace the clean accepted drawing. Its earlier 16-unit upward sparkle correction remains intact. The generic conversation metaphor is still a limitation. |
| Claude | Select the radial construction | Twelve unequal rays and an intentional central hub replace the sunrise-like arch and base. The full radial silhouette relates more closely to the app's burst cue and stays distinct from Gemini's four-point contour. It can still read as an asterisk or sun. |
| Perplexity | Select the axial scaffold | A full-height central axis, open angular branches and square shoulders replace the closed diamond and floating needle. The open regions survive the inspected small-size renders. Intersections are deliberate parts of the scaffold, not a claim about actual launcher masking. |

Only four SVG files change: `Clair/claude.svg`, `Obscur/claude.svg`, `Clair/perplexity.svg` and `Obscur/perplexity.svg`. The other 298 masters per appearance retain their exact 0.27.0 bytes. That includes ChatGPT, Gemini, Copilot and United Airlines. The qualified paired PNG export changes eight files across the two appearances and two export sizes, preserving the other 1,192 PNGs under the same renderer. All 27 earlier SVG archives remain reproducible and separate.

## Delivery and acceptance scope

Use the existing [paired gallery](https://t92t1914.github.io/clair-obscur-themes/#icons), SVG archive and individual PNG downloads. The [source exporter](samsung-icons.md#export-phone-images) retains each historical revision. The two [Android companions](android-iconpack.md) select their corresponding current appearance through the existing qualified export. Signed APKs remain on the private delivery route.

Vector, pixel, archive and compiled-resource checks answer different questions from human recognition and actual phone use. No recognition rate, participant count, reaction time, Samsung mask result or native persistence result was measured. The static icon assets are not adaptive-icon layers. Android's [adaptive-icon contract](https://developer.android.com/develop/ui/compose/system/icon_design_adaptive) does not establish how a particular launcher or Theme Park version will mask these assets.

Keyboard Companion 0.1.0 and its original backgrounds and platform recipes are unchanged. This revision does not install fonts, change autocorrection or apply a phone theme. Native installation together, assignment, both displays, masks, labels, switching, ordinary restart and restoration remain separate deferred checks.
